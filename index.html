What is the search space reduction rule for finding peak element in an unsorted array in O(log n)?

Compare `nums[mid]` with `nums[mid + 1]`. If `nums[mid] < nums[mid + 1]`, a peak MUST exist to the right (`left = mid + 1`). Otherwise, a peak exists at `mid` or to the left (`right = mid`).
